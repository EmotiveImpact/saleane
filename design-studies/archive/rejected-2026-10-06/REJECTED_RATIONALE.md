# SALEANE visual directions

**Review studies · 6 October 2026 · No direction is approved.**

These are three expressions of one brand, reconciled to the directions recovered from the original **Evaluate Saleane Name** conversation. They are not three alternative companies. The canonical spelling is **SALEANE**, without an accent.

The source proposed **A Quiet Luxury**, **B Modern Editorial** and **C After Dark**, with After Dark explicitly outside the primary operator interface. A later instruction strengthened the character: weight, permanence and institutional confidence, with operational density where needed. Both instructions govern these studies.

The original assistant favoured Quiet Luxury. That was a recommendation, not a user selection. The present recommendation is Modern Editorial for the operator workspace, with a test of Quiet Luxury’s hospitality qualities and an optional After Dark guest expression. This recommendation also remains unapproved.

## Controlled variables

A and B use the same fictional people, appointment states, appointment prices, metrics, content and interaction sequence. C uses Amelia’s next Skin Renewal Facial with Maya, the same service price and the same deposit concept. Money is illustrative; no payment, booking, message or persistent production record is created.

The studies have session-only state. A checkout or rebooking remains visible while switching A/B inside the page; a reload resets it. The full app’s persistent domain model is a separate artifact. The visual fixture does not prove inventory, communications, authentication, permissions, payment-provider or server behaviour.

## A · Quiet Luxury

**Expression:** warm ivory, stone, deep espresso, soft sage and generous space, held together by substantial operational structure.

| Role          | Value or decision                                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Deep espresso | `#40352D`: rail, primary actions and important headings                              |
| Warm ivory    | `#F4F2ED`: application ground                                                        |
| Stone         | `#DED9CE`: quiet but visible structural boundaries                                   |
| Soft sage     | `#E9EDE4`: people and service context                                                |
| Sand          | `#D6C7A9`: selected navigation and controlled emphasis                               |
| Type          | Manrope 650–800 for identity/headings; Source Sans 3 for work                        |
| Structure     | Stable left rail; four facts in one band; continuous agenda; distinct attention area |
| Density       | 78px desktop appointment rows, reduced on smaller screens                            |

The rail gives the workspace physical weight. Warmth is in the material palette, not decorative copy or ornate controls. The client’s history and money remain readable even when the brand mark is hidden. A geometric wordmark avoids turning a business operating system into a spa identity.

**Strength:** welcoming continuity with a premium independent studio; familiar wayfinding for operators.

**Risk:** the larger rail consumes working width, and cream/brown can become a generic hospitality treatment if paired with fashionable display decoration. Keep appointment states, controls and numbers crisp; avoid adding decorative cards or excessive softness.

## B · Modern Editorial

**Expression:** almost white, charcoal and muted nude; large, disciplined typography with a more compact operating rhythm. The original reference was premium skincare meeting financial software.

| Role         | Value or decision                                                                            |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Charcoal     | `#303537`: structural header, type, primary actions                                          |
| Almost white | `#F7F8F8`: application ground                                                                |
| White        | `#FFFFFF`: operational surfaces                                                              |
| Muted nude   | `#E7D6CB`: evidence-backed attention panel                                                   |
| Grey         | `#D9DFE0`: consistent boundaries and financial rows                                          |
| Type         | Manrope 700–800 for wordmark/headings; Source Sans 3 for operational detail                  |
| Structure    | Compact horizontal Today / Calendar / Clients / Inbox / Business navigation; broad work area |
| Density      | 64px desktop appointment rows; larger tap-friendly mobile rows                               |

The type is large where it establishes place and quiet where the user needs to work. The horizontal shell puts more width into the calendar and relationship timeline. A compact rectangular avatar and a continuous receipt support the desk’s transactional rhythm without turning every object into a card.

Appointment inspection uses a small right-side sheet. It holds the visit’s identity and next actions; the full relationship and checkout remain deliberate destinations. This follows the recovered source’s small-preview intent rather than exposing a large form just to inspect a booking.

**Strength:** strongest fit for the later request for institutional confidence, efficient operations and a compact top navigation. It gives the future calendar and connected relationship model room to grow.

**Risk:** coolness or overcompression. Retain clear visit status, useful preferences and humane typography; do not reduce hit targets simply to fit more appointments.

## C · After Dark

**Expression:** near-black brown, warm cream and champagne, reserved for a guest-facing or marketing expression.

| Role             | Value or decision                                                                                                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Near-black brown | `#251F1B`: guest background                                                                                                |
| Brown surface    | `#302822`: appointment review                                                                                              |
| Warm cream       | `#EEE6D8`: primary guest text                                                                                              |
| Champagne        | `#D7C4A3`: identity details and confirmation action                                                                        |
| Boundary brown   | `#51443A`: service and payment groupings                                                                                   |
| Type             | Manrope / Source Sans 3 for controls; system Georgia only for the guest editorial headline and fictional business monogram |
| Structure        | Business identity first; atmospheric introduction beside a bounded appointment review                                      |
| Density          | One service, time, practitioner, total and deposit, presented for a phone as well as desktop                               |

Atelier House is a fictional client brand, not a new Saleane mark. “With SALEANE” stays secondary. The abstract architectural illustration is original CSS geometry; no stock photography or generated imagery is presented as a real business asset.

**Strength:** distinctive guest hospitality and a controlled sense of occasion, without applying a dark treatment to an entire day’s operational work.

**Risk:** hierarchy and contrast become fragile if champagne is used for too much small text. The style is also business-dependent. A studio should retain its own identity, and the default booking experience must remain broadly suitable.

**Boundary:** this is not the recommended primary operator UI and does not establish a requirement for a general dark-mode product.

## Wordmark and typography

All directions spell **SALEANE** identically, with no accent, script letterform or decorative diacritic. The mark uses unmodified Manrope at weight 800 with compact but readable tracking: tighter in A, slightly more open in B. This tests visual emphasis while preserving spelling, search and cross-market typing.

The mark is a reviewable typographic treatment, not a finished custom logotype or trademark clearance. Its repeated A forms and broad geometric skeleton create weight. A later drawing pass can refine spacing and letter joins only after the direction is chosen.

Source Sans 3 provides practical small-text proportions. Both variable fonts are local, with their OFL notices copied alongside the study assets. Georgia is a system fallback used only for C’s guest editorial layer; no commercial font purchase or licence is implied. These choices keep the software legible and inspectable while brand decisions remain open.

## Recommendation and selection criteria

Start the next operator refinement from **B Modern Editorial**, subject to review. Its horizontal structure and clearer work surface fit the source architecture and the later demand for a weighted, resilient product. Test whether A’s warmer surfaces improve hospitality without slowing the same tasks. Keep C as an optional guest expression.

Compare using real task questions:

1. Find the person who has arrived, open the visit and recover a useful preference.
2. Explain the price, deposit, gratuity and remaining balance without guessing.
3. Finish the visit and give the next appointment a place.
4. Recover the next visit from the client timeline.
5. Explain an intelligence suggestion and identify its source without treating it as an action already taken.

Preference, task completion, reading effort and error recovery should guide selection. No customer study has yet validated these directions. A visual preference does not override the product contract or establish production readiness.

All had zero horizontal document overflow and zero violations in the selected axe WCAG 2 A/AA, 2.1 AA and 2.2 AA rule tags. The UK checkout calculated £84.25 after a 15% tip and £25 deposit; the US checkout calculated $125 after a 20% tip and $25 deposit. Rebooking preserved the selected 11:00 time in the timeline, A/B switching preserved the session state, Escape closed the appointment preview and restored its trigger, and the mobile After Dark guest confirmation passed. No JavaScript page errors occurred in this pass. Earlier contrast findings were fixed before this recorded pass.

Automated checks and these visual inspections are review evidence, not a WCAG certification, a user-research result or production acceptance. The original cloud visual files were not used by this visual agent; the textual directions recovered by the parent agent supplied the source constraint.
