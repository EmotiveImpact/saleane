# Photographs for SALEANE: prompts for an image tool

SALEANE has a small library of house photographs (`src/design/photos.ts`), and many screens share the same few. Nothing is missing today. These prompts make a fresh set, one for each screen that still borrows a library photograph, with a good mix of people. Each new photograph replaces the borrowed one on its screen when it arrives.

## How to use them

- ChatGPT makes one picture at a time. Paste the message under "Ready to paste into ChatGPT" at the end of this page, then say "next" after each picture. Every prompt below also works on its own.
- Three shapes: **tall** is portrait 2:3 (1600 × 2400), **wide** is landscape 3:2 (2400 × 1600) and **square** is 1:1 (1600 × 1600). Ask for the largest size the tool allows. They are resized and cropped to fit here.
- Each prompt says where the subject sits and which part stays plain, because the studio's name or the page's words sit over some of these pictures and wide ones are often shown as a thin band.
- Download each picture, name it with the file name given (PNG is fine) and send them in the project chat, in any batches. They are converted to WebP, saved in `public/images/library/` under the folder named for each group, and put on their screen.
- All of them are fictional and AI-made, like the rest of the library. They are never shown as a real studio's premises or people.

## Screens with no photograph

The moving-in wizard, the first weeks list on Today and the add-a-location window are short, practical steps with no photograph, so they need no prompts.

## Three photographs that only need saving again

`treatments/body-wrap`, `treatments/lash-lift` and `moments/guest-arriving` in the photo library pull request came through as empty files. Their original pictures are fine (`design-studies/photo-originals/` on the photo library branch), so they need saving again from those originals, not making again. ## The house style (already inside every prompt)

Editorial photography for a calm, premium beauty and wellness studio. Bright, airy and sunlit, with soft natural daylight through linen or sheer curtains. Warm off-white walls, limewash plaster, pale oak, travertine, linen, ceramic and dried botanicals, with touches of muted sage green and dusty rose. Light, uncluttered and serene, with gentle shadows. Shot on a medium-format camera with a 50mm lens and shallow depth of field, in natural colour. Real, natural-looking people, never posed stock-photo smiles. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks, no readable screens. Not dark, moody, candlelit, neon or night-time.
