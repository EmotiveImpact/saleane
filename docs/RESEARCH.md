## Evidence → design/build decision

| Source                                                                                                                                                                | Observed pattern                                                                                                                                                        | Saleane decision                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Mangomint scheduling](https://www.mangomint.com/features/scheduling/)                                                                                                | Calendar, checkout, buffers and scarce rooms/resources are connected; rebooking is part of the calendar workflow.                                                       | One visit sheet across Today and Calendar. Availability reserves professional and room for treatment plus turnaround. Checkout leads directly to a linked next visit. |
| [Mangomint client management](https://www.mangomint.com/features/client-management/)                                                                                  | Client details and sale/appointment context share a relationship surface.                                                                                               | A durable client ID and event timeline connect visits, receipt and referral edges. No copied “CRM contacts” table.                                                    |
| [Mangomint online booking](https://www.mangomint.com/features/online-booking/)                                                                                        | Booking is a customer-facing entry into the same operational schedule.                                                                                                  | Branded service → professional/time → details → review flow; available slots come from the same rules as desk booking.                                                |
| [Boulevard scheduling](https://www.joinblvd.com/features/scheduling), [client profiles](https://www.joinblvd.com/features/client-management)                          | Its public pages position scheduling and client profiles within one management product. The fetched bodies contained navigation rather than a full product walkthrough. | Useful category confirmation only. No claimed account access, usability test or detailed benchmark from these pages.                                                  |
| [Radix Dialog](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/dialog), [accessibility overview](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/overview/accessibility) | Low-level components provide dialog semantics, keyboard behaviour and focus management while allowing custom composition.                                               | Wrap the primitive in Saleane’s own `Modal`, tokens and visual layer. Do not adopt a generic theme.                                                                   |
| [WAI modal-dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/)                                                                                    | A modal constrains focus and restores it to a logical initiating place.                                                                                                 | Label every dialog, test Tab/Escape and explicitly restore the initiating control when there is no Radix Trigger.                                                     |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)                                                                                                                             | Accessibility spans keyboard, focus, readable contrast and target interaction, not colour alone.                                                                        | Native buttons, labelled controls, textual state, agenda alternative and chart data table; AA is a future release target.                                             |
| [Manrope font log](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/FONTLOG.txt)                                                                                 | Geometric open-source family with variable weight history.                                                                                                              | Substantial weight-800 wordmark with compact spacing; no accent or glyph modification in the prototype.                                                               |
| [Adobe Source Sans](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans)                                                                                                       | Sans-serif family intended for UI environments.                                                                                                                         | Use Source Sans 3 for functional text and Manrope for headings/mark. Local packages and OFL notices verified in installed files.                                      |
| [Vite guide](https://vite.dev/guide/)                                                                                                                                 | A documented development/build pipeline supports a maintainable browser application.                                                                                    | Vite + React + TypeScript, lockfile, lint, tests and a reproducible production bundle.                                                                                |

These are design inferences from the sources, not proof that Saleane outperforms those products. No competitor screenshots or proprietary UI assets were copied into the application.

## UK/US boundary research

| Primary source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | What it establishes                                                                                                               | Product consequence                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [ICO electronic-mail marketing rules](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/) | Consent is normally required for unsolicited electronic marketing to individual subscribers, with specific exceptions/conditions. | Marketing and appointment updates are separate purposes. Demo email opt-in defaults off. Production needs channel/purpose/version/source/withdrawal records and a jurisdiction-reviewed policy. |
| [HHS covered entities and business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html)                                                                                                                                   | HIPAA scope depends on covered-entity/business-associate status, rather than the word “spa” alone.                                | Clinical requires an entity/jurisdiction assessment and appropriate contracts; core Saleane makes no HIPAA-compliance claim.                                                                    |
| [FTC Health Breach Notification Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/health-breach-notification-rule)                                                                                                                                     | Certain personal-health-record vendors and related entities have breach-notification obligations.                                 | Being outside HIPAA does not finish a health-data assessment. Keep clinical intake and health-data inference out of core until reviewed.                                                        |

These sources identify work and boundaries. They do not replace legal advice. Tax classifications, cancellation/deposit rights, US SMS/calling rules, regional privacy and retention need qualified review before a real pilot. The FCC overview URL attempted in this pass did not load; no current US telemarketing rule is asserted from it.

## Choices still to test with people

| Hypothesis                                                       | Validation                                                                                   | Stop/change signal                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Today as an operating brief reduces front-desk switching         | Observe 5 UK and 5 US practitioners/receptionists booking, welcoming, settling and rebooking | Operators cannot find the next action or trust the status       |

| A client timeline supports relationship ownership                | Ask users to recover an old preference, receipt and referral connection                      | Context is buried or permission boundaries feel unclear         |
