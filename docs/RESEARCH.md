### Audience and customer jobs

Initial ICP: premium independent beauty, spa and wellness businesses with **1–10 practitioners and 1–2 locations**. A proposed first test cohort is a 2–5-person studio sharing rooms, alongside solo operators to prevent unnecessary complexity. Hair is supported, but large hair salons do not dictate every interaction. Medical practices are outside core launch.

The NHBF's published 2025 statistics section describes 50,400 VAT/PAYE-registered UK hair/beauty businesses, with 81% employing fewer than five people and 96% fewer than ten. This supports designing for limited administrative capacity; it is not a count of Saleane's addressable paying customers. [NHBF industry statistics](https://www.nhbf.co.uk/about-the-nhbf/campaigning-for-you/industry-research-reports-and-statistics/nhbf-industry-statistics/).

| Job / trigger                     | What must happen                                                                 | Failure worth measuring                                      | Requirement / research task                     |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| A client is standing at reception | Find their next visit, ordinary preferences, balance and source history quickly  | Wrong identity, excessive switching, exposed private data    | CLI-01/02; timed known-client lookup            |
| Tomorrow has a gap                | Find genuinely eligible clients and capacity, then review a permitted invitation | Invented revenue, unsuitable time, unwanted marketing        | INT-01, COM-01; owner reviews suggested matches |
| A client cancels                  | Release both staff and room, apply policy, offer recovery                        | Room stays blocked, automatic unfair fee, duplicate outreach | CAL-03, PAY-03; cancellation/recovery task      |
| Someone has not returned          | Explain the evidence and distinguish cadence from certainty                      | Generic interval labels regular long-cycle clients as lapsed | CLI-05; compare service and client cadence      |
| The month is below target         | Separate booked, earned, collected, tax and tips and show contributors           | Unexplained totals or forecasts presented as cash            | BIZ-01/03; owner reconciles receipts            |
| Move from an incumbent            | Preview mapping and reconcile counts, future visits and balances                 | Silent loss, duplicate identity, false consent               | MIG-01; permissioned source-export rehearsal    |
| Internet fails during a visit     | Read the last authorised daily snapshot and call for assistance                  | Silent stale state, unsafe offline payment/booking           | OFF-01; offline and reconnect exercise          |

These jobs are derived from the conversation and documented workflows. Recruit five UK and five US participants across owner, desk, practitioner and solo roles; test booking, cancellation, checkout, history lookup and reporting with the same scenario. Record task time, errors, help requests and confidence, then revise the PRD. Do not claim representative demand from anecdotes or substitute vendor marketing for field observation.

### Payments decision and qualification plan

Recommend evaluating Stripe Connect for hosted/tokenised entry and connected merchant operations, against Square where the pilot already relies on its POS. This is an evaluation recommendation, not a provider selection or deployment. Record merchant of record, account ownership, payout recipient, refunds/disputes, fee liability, terminal needs, countries, support and data residency before choosing a charge flow.

Stripe's UK standard-card online rate is advertised at 1.5% + 20p; card category, international/FX and platform services can change costs. Do not model payment revenue as the difference between that headline and a proposed customer rate. [Stripe UK pricing](https://stripe.com/gb/pricing), [Connect pricing](https://stripe.com/connect/pricing), [charge-flow consequences](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges), [Square Payments API](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/payments-api/take-payments).

Qualification must prove: held slot → deposit success/failure → lost redirect recovery → signed/deduplicated webhook → late capture after expiry → checkout applying deposit once → partial/full refund → dispute and payout reconciliation. Use provider-hosted card collection; store tokens and references, never card numbers. Provider sandbox evidence precedes supervised live tests and cannot substitute for them. [Stripe security integration guide](https://docs.stripe.com/security/guide).

### Migration contract

MIG-01 owns a staged process: source inventory → scoped export → dry-run mapping → rejected/duplicate review → financial opening balances → rehearsal → cutover → reconciliation → retained source access and rollback window. Start with clients/services/staff/resources/future appointments, then classify historical visits and money. Do not imply that every vendor exports all of these.

Keep `(organisation, source system, source record ID)` mappings. An idempotent rerun cannot duplicate a client or visit. Show row-level errors with recoverable source references; validate venue time zones, practitioner capability, resource overlap, currency and totals. Unknown marketing permission imports as unknown/off; no inferred opt-in. Exclude clinical records. Do not transfer card data or silently promise card-on-file continuity. Gift cards, packages and deposits need separately reconciled opening liabilities; unsupported liabilities block live cutover, not disappear into notes.

Pilot drill: compare source/destination counts, future-visit allocation, contacts, consent and sampled historical records; reconcile deposit/liability totals by currency; export a signed-off exception report. Freeze writes for final delta, identify the accountable business owner, rehearse rollback and retain an immutable import manifest. Migration success is reconciliation, not a green upload toast.
