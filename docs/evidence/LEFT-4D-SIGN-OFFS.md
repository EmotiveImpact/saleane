# LEFT-4D · Sign-offs for refunds, prices and the rota

Built 10 October 2026 in a worktree, on top of main at `7684b33`. Everything below was checked with unit, component and server tests. It was not opened in a browser in this build: the brief for this part said not to start a dev server or a browser, so the lead's browser steps are at the end.

## What it does

- **The owner's rules.** On Requests, the owner chooses Sign-off rules: what a general manager can give back on one receipt without a sign-off, which of a general manager's rota changes need one (none, extra days, or every change) and, once the studio has regions, what a regional manager can approve. A limit for managers is rarely wanted, so it sits under More options. Empty means no limit. The rules show under the Requests heading in a sentence each, so general managers and managers see their own limits too. The rules save together as one change, kept in Who changed what.
- **Refunds over a limit.** The limit counts everything given back on that receipt, so splitting a refund does not get round it. Over it, the refund is not carried out: the desk sees "This is over the £50.00 you can give back without a sign-off. Ask for a sign-off from the receipt first." (or "This would take the money given back on this receipt to £60.00, over the £50.00 ..."). The server refuses it in the same words, for a refund recorded by hand, a walk-in return and a card refund alike.
- **Asking from the receipt.** On a visit receipt, a deposit or fee refund to the card, and a walk-in sale's Give money back, someone with a limit sees what they can give back and Ask for a sign-off: the amount (up to what is left) and why the money goes back. The request keeps the receipt number, what was sold, what it could give back and what had gone back already, never the client. A reason that names the client (any part of their name of three letters or more, their email or the last seven digits of their phone) is refused: "Leave the client’s name and details out of the reason. The person deciding sees the receipt, not the client." It is routed by the existing rules: the location's regional manager within their limit, otherwise the owner.
- **Once approved.** Approving moves no money: it says so on the request ("Approving moves no money. Gia can then give back £60.00 from this receipt once, within 7 days."). The receipt then shows "Approved by Rae Area: give back exactly £60.00, once, from this receipt, by Tuesday 13 October." and a Give back £60.00 button that opens the refund with that amount filled in. The refund is then recorded, or sent to the card in test mode, exactly as before. The sign-off is used by the refund it allowed and never again. It does not cover another receipt or another amount ("The sign-off for this receipt is for exactly £60.00. Give back that amount, or ask again."). It lapses after 7 of the studio's days, or as soon as anything else is given back on that receipt, and then a new request can be raised. A receipt also says when a refund is waiting, was declined (with the word back) or was given back.
- **New prices.** A general manager chooses Ask for a sign-off on Requests, then A new price: the treatment (each shown with its price at their location now), the new price and the day it starts (today up to a year ahead, chosen from the studio's calendar field). Once approved, the price changes at that location on that day by itself: straight away when the day has come, otherwise the server starts it in the first minute of that day on the studio's own clock. It is checked again then; if it can no longer start (the treatment left the menu, the location closed, the price was changed by hand meanwhile, a house rule, a deposit above the price), the request says why and nothing changes. Visits already booked keep the price they were booked at. Who changed what shows "Started a new price that was signed off", made by SALEANE, with the price before and after.
- **Rota changes.** A request can carry one day on the rota for one person (`rota-day` or `rota-day-usual`, exactly as it would be saved), checked when it is raised and again when it is approved. It must be a day at the request's location, before or after. Approving runs that exact command through `src/domain/rota.ts`, so the rota changes as soon as it is approved, kept in Who changed what as made by whoever approved it. If the rota has changed so the day cannot be saved, approving is refused in plain words and the request can be declined instead. The owner's rule decides which general manager rota changes need a sign-off (see The seam with the general manager rota below).
- **The request screens.** Requests (owners, managers, general managers) and the regional manager's area page show each request with what is asked in one line ("SL-41 · Skin Renewal Facial and 2 products · £60.00 back of £171.00 paid", "£95.00 now, £105.00 from Sun 1 Nov", "Theo · Mon 12 Oct · not working now, 10:00 to 16:00 asked"), the reason, where and when it was asked and by whom, who it is waiting on and why it went to them (with regions), and what approving will do. Decide opens the request line by line, before and after (Receipt, For, Paid, Given back before, To give back; Treatment, Price now, New price, Starts; Who, Day, Now, Asked for), then Approve or Decline with an optional word back. Decided requests keep who decided and when, the word back, and what came of it: "Given back on Wednesday 7 October.", "Lapsed: it was not given back by Tuesday 13 October.", "Starts by itself on Sunday 1 November.", "The new price started on ...", "The rota shows the change."
- **Who decides.** As before: the location's regional manager within their limit, otherwise the owner; the owner can decide any. General managers and managers never decide. Nobody decides a request they raised: one raised by a region's own manager passes up to the owner, and the server refuses them if they try. A regional manager asks for spending or anything else only; refunds, prices and rota days are asked for by the location's team, who can see the receipt, the menu and the rota.
- **Regional managers never see clients.** Their area view carries the request as kept: receipt number, amount, treatment, prices before and after, a team member's first name and their hours. The server test reads their whole view and finds none of the guest's name, email, phone or record id.
- **Requests tab.** Requests now also shows in a studio of one location once anything has been asked or the owner has set sign-off rules (the sample studio has both). Before that it stays hidden, as before.
- **Help.** New guides: Ask for a sign-off on a refund (managers and general managers), Ask for a new price (general managers), Set refund limits and sign-off rules (owner). Ask for a sign-off and Refund a payment say what is new. Search checks find each from the words people use ("refund over my limit", "goodwill refund approval", "put up the price of a treatment", "refund limit", "extra days need a sign-off").
- **Sample studio.** General managers give back up to £50 on a receipt and extra rota days need a sign-off. Three requests wait for the owner, raised by Nadia Ross: a £60 goodwill refund on a recent facial's receipt, a £10 rise for the Skin Renewal Facial from Sunday 1 November, and an extra Monday for Theo (10:00 to 16:00 on 12 October). The sample never charges, refunds or sends anything; approving the refund in the sample moves nothing. A sample studio made on the server has the requests at its own location. The browser demo's real-clock London studio, which leaves out the sample's receipts, keeps the price and rota requests (moved onto its days, and asked the morning before when the clock is earlier) and leaves out the refund request with its receipt.

## Server

- **No migration.** Requests and rules live in the studio record, as V5's did. New request fields: `refund`, `price`, `rota`, `carriedOut` and `notStarted`; new rules: `generalRefundLimitMinor`, `managerRefundLimitMinor`, `rotaSignOff`. New commands: `signoff-rules` (owner only, through the `approval-limit` permission) and `request-start` (never accepted from anyone; only the server's own job sends it).
- `POST /commands`: for a general manager or manager, `signOffRefusal` runs after a replayed request is answered and the revision is checked, so a replay of an approved refund returns its first answer rather than being refused because the sign-off is now used. A refund over the limit is also refused with 409 while a card refund on that receipt is under way or unclear, so one sign-off cannot be used twice at once.
- `POST /card-refunds`: the same limit check before anything is sent to the payment provider. The sign-off is marked used in the same change that records the refund once the provider confirms it, judged as of when the desk started the refund, so a refund started on its last day still uses it.
- `server/signoffs.ts`: once a minute (like billing), studios holding an approved price that has neither started nor been stopped are read with one query, and each due price is started through `request-start` in its own transaction, written with `ctx.persist` and no actor, so Who changed what names SALEANE. `api.signOffs.run()` is used by the tests with a moving clock.
- `server/workspace.ts`: a sample studio made on the server puts the sample's requests at its own location, as it does its branch visit.
- Every check is in `src/domain/signoffs.ts`; the screens use the same functions to say what will happen before anyone presses a button.

## The seam with the general manager rota (Stage 4, item 3)

Item 3 gives general managers rota rights in parallel; this part did not build their rota screens. To join up:

1. `rotaSignOffNeeded(state, command, now)` says whether a general manager's `rota-day` or `rota-week-copy` needs a sign-off under the owner's rule (`'extra'`: a working day the person does not usually work; `'all'`: every rota command).
2. The server already calls it: `signOffRefusal` in `server/routes/workspace.ts` refuses such a command from a general manager with "This change to the rota needs a sign-off. Choose Ask for a sign-off to send it up." It is dormant today only because general managers cannot send rota commands at all; it applies the moment item 3 lets them.
3. Item 3's day dialog, when `rotaSignOffNeeded` is true for a general manager, offers Ask for a sign-off and sends `{ type: 'request-raise', input: { locationId, kind: 'rota', rota: <the rota-day or rota-day-usual command it would have saved>, note? } }`. Copying a week is refused with words asking for each day on its own.
4. Approval runs the command through `runRotaCommand`; nothing else is needed.

## Decisions

- **Seven of the studio's days** for an approved refund, ending at midnight on the studio's clock. Long enough to reach the client, short enough that an old approval is not lying about.
- **Exactly the approved amount.** A smaller refund needs a new request. This keeps "used once for that amount" simple to check and to explain.
- **The limit counts the receipt's earlier refunds**, so two £40 refunds on one receipt need a sign-off under a £50 limit.
- **Anything else given back on the receipt ends the approval**, since the receipt is no longer what was approved. A refund of the approved amount by anyone, the owner included, uses it.
- **Card refunds** use the existing card refund path in test mode; the limit is checked before the provider is asked, and the approval is used when the provider confirms.
- **Prices at the main location** change the house price, as the treatment form does; every other location that offers it keeps the price it had (written as its own location price). At another location, its location price is set or dropped when it matches the house price. House rules and deposits are checked when asked, and again on the day.
- **A price for today starts on approval**; a later one starts in the server's first minute of that day. In the browser-only sample, whose day never moves, a later price waits.
- **Who changed what**: a refund records its own change as before; approving a price or rota change records what it changed under the person who approved it; a price starting later is recorded as made by SALEANE.
- **Rota requests** are one day for one person at the request's location. Copying or clearing a week is not a request.
- **Regional managers ask for spending or anything else only**, since they do not see receipts, menus or the rota.
- **The owner is the last word**: a request the owner raises is theirs to decide. Everyone else never decides their own.
- **Membership and gift card cancellations** that give money back are not under the refund limit. General managers cannot cancel either; managers can, as before, with no limit. The brief named refunds from a sale; bringing those two under the managers' limit would be a small follow-up.
- **No migration**, so nothing to renumber when this merges.

## Quality bar

- **One main job and one primary button.** Requests keeps Ask for a sign-off as its one primary button; Sign-off rules is secondary. On a receipt, Ask for a sign-off and Give back £60.00 are secondary beside the existing refund button; the ask form has one primary, Send for sign-off, with Not now as a quiet partner. Decide keeps Approve as primary and Decline secondary.
- **Rarely used options tucked away.** The managers' refund limit sits under More options in Sign-off rules. `MoreOptions` is not in `src/design` on main, so this is a native `<details>` styled like the rota's Change the whole week fold; it opens by itself when a managers' limit is already set. It can move to `MoreOptions` when that merges.
- **No card inside a card.** Requests are rows with hairlines. A receipt's sign-off sits under a hairline in the receipt panel, not in a box; the decide dialog uses the receipt's own rows for before and after.
- **Photographs.** None added: these are working surfaces.
- **Empty pages.** Requests keeps its existing empty state, now mentioning refunds and prices. No new page can be empty.
- **Loading.** `LoadingShapes` is not in `src/design` on main. Nothing new loads on its own: requests arrive with the studio record. Buttons say "Sending…" while a request is sent.
- **Motion.** `--motion-calm` is not in `src/design` on main, so this adds no motion.
- **390 px.** Forms use the existing two-column fields that stack on phones; on phones the sign-off and receipt buttons run full width at the foot of their form; long receipt lines wrap. Not yet seen in a browser (see below).
- **Words.** Plain British English, "visit" and "treatment", no exclamation marks and no dashes. Nothing says money moved when a sign-off is approved: "Approving moves no money", "Sent for a sign-off. Nothing has been given back yet."
- **Built from the library.** `Button`, `Modal`, `Empty`, `ErrorMessage`, `PageHeader` and `DateField` from `src/design`, tokens only, no new colours, no left-edge stripes. One small stylesheet, `src/components/signoffs.css`.
- **Before and after pictures**: not taken in this build; the lead's browser pass below covers them.

## Limits and notes

- In a one-location studio with nothing asked and no rules, Requests stays hidden, so its owner reaches Sign-off rules from Help's Go there (or `/business/requests`). General managers only exist once there is a second location, so this matters only for a managers' limit.
- A regional manager's area page works out an approved refund's last day from the date it was approved, without the studio's clock or its other refunds, so it shows the approval's terms; the studio's own pages show exactly where it stands.
- The sample studio's requests come from Nadia Ross, a fictional general manager who is not on the sample team list.

## Browser steps for the lead

At 1440 px and 390 px, against the local server:

1. Sign up as a new owner with the sample studio. Business shows a Requests tab. Open it: three requests wait, each with its line, reason, "asked ... by Nadia Ross", "Waiting on the owner." and what approving does. The rules show under the heading.
2. Choose Decide on the refund: the dialog lists Receipt, For, Paid, Given back before and To give back, says approving moves no money. Approve. The Decided list says "Can be given back once, from the receipt, until Tuesday 13 October."
3. Choose Decide on the new price: Treatment, Price now, New price, Starts Sunday 1 November. Approve: it says it starts by itself on Sunday 1 November; the price in Settings, Treatments has not changed.
4. Choose Decide on Theo's rota: Now not working, Asked for 10:00 to 16:00 (an extra day). Approve: Rota, week of 12 October, shows Theo working Monday 10:00 to 16:00, and Who changed what shows the change by the owner.
5. Choose Sign-off rules: change the general managers' limit to 40, rota to Every change, open More options and set managers to 20. Save; the sentences under the heading change. Who changed what lists "Changed the sign-off rules".
6. Add a second location, group both into a region, then invite a general manager for Marylebone (the main location, where the sample's receipts are) and a regional manager for that region (Settings, Team). As the general manager: Money, open a receipt: "You can give back up to £40.00 ...". Record a refund over 40: the error names the limit. Choose Ask for a sign-off, give a reason with the client's first name: refused; without it: "Sent for a sign-off. Nothing has been given back yet."
7. As the regional manager: the area page's Requests shows the refund with receipt number, amount and treatment and no client. Decide, Approve.
8. As the general manager: the receipt says "Approved by ...: give back exactly £60.00 ..." with Give back £60.00, which opens the refund with 60.00 filled in. Record it (test card or simulated). Try again: refused. Requests shows "Given back on ...".
9. As the general manager: Requests, Ask for a sign-off, Kind A new price, pick a treatment, enter a price and a start day. Send. As the regional manager approve it; as the owner, Who changed what shows it when its day comes (or approve one for today and see the price change at once).
10. Repeat steps 1, 2, 6 and 8 at 390 px: nothing scrolls sideways, buttons are full width at the foot of the ask form, and the decide dialog's rows wrap.
