# NEXT-12 · Talking to Ask SALEANE (part 2)

Recorded 10 October 2026 on a local build (Chromium, 127.0.0.1:5173). A studio of its own, not a sample, with Rosa Field booked that day. The server was started with Claude and OpenAI's voice switched on, and a pretend Anthropic and a pretend OpenAI in place of the real ones, kept outside the repository: the pretend OpenAI answers the browser's offer, then hears "Who is in today?" a moment after the talk starts and hands it over, as GPT-Live-1's client delegation documents. The browser's microphone and WebRTC connection were stood in for, as a test browser has neither. Everything else (the voice layer, the conversation, the metering, the routes and the screens) is the code in this change.

## What it does

`SALEANE_VOICE_PROVIDER=openai` with `OPENAI_API_KEY`, alongside Claude from part 1, adds a Talk button to Ask SALEANE. OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 listens and speaks; Claude does the thinking, with the same skills, permissions and approvals as a typed question. The browser connects to OpenAI over WebRTC with an offer SALEANE passes on, so the key never reaches the browser. SALEANE follows the talk on its own line to OpenAI: when the voice hands a question over, SALEANE says quietly that it is looking at the studio records, asks Claude, and gives the voice the answer to say. A change Claude prepares waits on screen for Approve or Not now, as a typed one does. The voice says it is an AI, speaks for the studio in British or American English, and never claims a change was made.

Talking time is metered as the price promises: before a talk starts SALEANE holds up to ten minutes (or what is left of the month, if less) and their worst cost; fewer than 30 seconds left is refused plainly. SALEANE closes the talk when its time is up, counts the seconds OpenAI reports (or its own clock if OpenAI goes quiet), and gives the rest back. Each spoken question costs what Claude charged and is not counted as a typed question. A talk left open is closed when the same person starts another, a dropped line is followed once to close it, and nobody else can see or end someone's talk. Unset, there is no Talk button and nothing is sent. A sample studio never talks.

## Browser journey

1. With both switched on, Ask SALEANE says "Answers come from your studio records, read by Claude, an AI from Anthropic. Your question and the records needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic. Changes wait for your approval. When you talk, your voice goes to OpenAI, which listens and speaks the answers Claude gives." (worded so after review: the walk saw the earlier "Changes wait for your approval, and nothing is sent. When you talk, OpenAI's voice listens and speaks, and Claude answers.", which did not say that the records go to Anthropic and the voice to OpenAI; `askSaleaneTalk.test.tsx` checks the new words) A Talk button sits beside Ask (only its microphone on a phone), and the meter reads "0 of 300 questions used this month. 0 of 30 talking minutes used."
2. Tapping Talk shows "Talking · 10:00 left", counting down, with Stop. "Who is in today?" appeared with a microphone as what was said, and Claude's answer, "You have 1 visit today. Rosa Field at 10:00.", appeared below it.
3. "Remind me to call Rosa tomorrow about her patch test." came back as "I have prepared a reminder for tomorrow to call Rosa. It is waiting on your screen to approve." with "Remind the team on Sunday 11 October: Call Rosa about her patch test" and Approve or Not now. Approving it showed "Done. Saved to your records." and saved the reminder.
4. Stop ended the talk: "The talk has ended." and the meter read "1 of 30 talking minutes used." The typed questions stayed at 0 of 300: spoken ones are counted as talking time. After a talk at each width, the studio's usage showed 26 seconds of talking.
5. With the microphone refused, Ask SALEANE said "SALEANE needs your microphone to talk. Allow it in your browser, or type instead." and the typed question box carried on as before.

axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) found nothing on Ask SALEANE ready to talk, talking, with a suggestion, after the talk and with the microphone refused, each at 1440 px and 390 px. There was no sideways scroll at either width and there were no console errors. The listening dot pulses only for people who have not asked their device for reduced motion.

## What it costs

GPT-Live-1 is $0.05 a minute, billed by the second, with 15 seconds billed to start a session (OpenAI's price on 10 October 2026). The 30 talking minutes in each location's price come to $1.50 a month at most, plus Claude's thinking for each spoken question (about 1 to 2 cents, as a typed one). A talk is held at its worst, ten minutes and fifteen seconds (51.25 cents), against the day's ceiling before it starts, so no talk can start that the day could not pay for. These figures are worked out from OpenAI's published price, not measured on a real account.

## Not covered

Not run against a real OpenAI or Anthropic account: no key was used, and the voice layer follows OpenAI's published GPT-Live-1 API with simulated answers. Real speech, the microphone and audio were not exercised; the browser's WebRTC connection was stood in for. Safari and Firefox were not checked. Messages, card payments and everything else stay in test mode.
