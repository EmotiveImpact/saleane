# Whole app review and fixes: evidence

Recorded 9 October 2026 in Chromium (Playwright) against the dev server on 127.0.0.1:5173 with its local database. Three kinds of studio were used: a brand new one, "Linden Rooms" (UK, Clerkenwell), created on the account page as an owner would; a signed-in sample studio, "Fernleaf Rooms"; and the signed-out fictional demo. All names, numbers, emails and addresses are fictional. The review list itself, with the before screenshots, is in the project files under `app-review-2026-10-09`.

## What was exercised

**A brand new studio (items 5, 28, 29).** Creating "Linden Rooms" on the account page made a UK studio closed on Sunday with online booking off. Today showed "Get your studio ready" with eight steps, VAT among them, each linking to its place in Settings. Its booking page said "This booking page isn't available". A new tab opened at `/clients` stayed in Linden Rooms ("Linden Rooms · SALEANE"), not the demo. After a treatment (a £95 facial with a £20 deposit), Maya, a treatment room, VAT at 20%, a 50% late cancellation fee, arrival notes, the studio's phone and email and online booking were set through the same commands Settings sends, the list read 7 of 8 done, leaving only the opening hours to confirm. A second brand new studio, opened in the same way, showed the list again with what SALEANE noticed across the full width under the day, as there was no special to show.

**A guest books (items 2, 3, 4, 6, 41, 42).** On the booking page the day strip started on the studio's today and offered nothing earlier; "First available" chose 10:45 that morning. The phone number typed the local way, "07700 900456", was kept as "+44 7700 900456". The review step showed the treatment price, the £20 deposit and £75 left to pay, with "Deposit and cancellation" spelling out the rule before the guest confirmed: for a visit less than 24 hours away, the studio keeps the deposit and may charge up to 50% of the price, less the deposit. A second guest booking for Monday 12 October at 14:00 was told it was free to cancel until Sunday 11 October at 14:00. The confirmation offered "Pay your £20.00 deposit now", "View my visit", "Add to calendar" and "Copy link", and repeated the summary with the address, the studio's phone and "Before you arrive". At 390px the booking page had no sideways scroll.

**The guest's own visit page (items 7, 12, 31, 40).** The visit page showed the studio's address with directions, phone and email. For the visit that morning it explained that it was too near to move online. For the Monday visit, "Move my visit" offered a day strip and times; choosing Saturday 10 October at 09:00 showed "Now" and "New time" side by side with "This new time is less than 24 hours away, so after this your visit can't be moved online again." Confirming said "Your visit has moved to Saturday 10 October at 09:00 with Maya. Linden Rooms can see the change." Paying the deposit on the test payment page ended with "This was a test of the £20.00 deposit, so no money was taken and no card was charged." A made-up link, `/manage#not-a-real-link`, said "This link has expired or been replaced. Ask the studio for a new one."

**The AI receptionist on the booking page (item 18).** Asked "Are you open on Sunday?" it answered "We are closed on Sunday 11 October." Asked "Any facial times tomorrow afternoon?" it listed Saturday's facial times, each one a button (12 in all).

**Team and places (items 11, 13, 30).** An Islington location and a "North London" region were added, and reception and regional manager invitations made. The join page named Linden Rooms, the role ("Reception: Looks after bookings, clients and checkout.") and filled in the invited email. The new receptionist landed on Business, opening on Money, with Money, Stock, Rota and Gift cards. The regional manager, Rae, was greeted "Good morning, Rae." on their own page and could sign out from it.

**Settings and Business (items 14, 15, 19, 37).** Settings showed five groups: Studio, Team, Treatments and money, Guests and messages, and Your account and data. The team access panel had room around its fields. In the real studio an Instagram account added by its owner showed "Waiting to be confirmed". (The panel is not shown for sample studios, so the first attempt in the sample studio found nothing to fill; it was repeated in Linden Rooms.) Business on a 390px phone kept its tabs on one row that scrolls, with no sideways scroll of the page.

**The fictional demo (items 24, 27, 32, 33, 36, 43).** Today showed the sample day at 10:45 with 12 visits, "Needs you" and "Your team today" in the right-hand column, and what SALEANE noticed and one special side by side under the day. The page is 2,448px tall at 1440px wide, against 6,054px in the review's before screenshot. The visit panel opened for the first visit. The rebooking date read "Tue 3 Nov 2026". Gift cards showed as cards. axe found nothing on Calendar, Inbox, Stock, Gift cards, Settings and Business. The price page's wordmark stays on `/pricing`, and its card and text features read "Arrives when you connect your card account" or "text account".

There were no console errors in any of these runs.

## Every page

**Accessibility and fit.** `scripts/qualify.mjs` opened every staff page at 1280, 390 and 320px, in the signed-out demo and in a signed-in sample studio, plus the guest booking page and the account and price pages: 165 pages with no axe violations (WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules and best practice), no sideways scroll and a visible focus on everything reached with Tab. The keyboard-only daily loop (open a visit, welcome, check out, rebook) and the main dialogs (search, a visit, New visit, a measure's definition) passed in both. The only console errors are the expected 401s on the signed-out demo's account page, as on main. A first run found a skipped heading level on an empty Requests page and the Accounts page scrolling sideways on a phone; both are fixed and the second run is clean.

**Pages loading when first opened (item 46).** Each page now brings its own styles. Every page above was opened on its own at 1280 and 390px and checked against every stylesheet in the app: any rule from a stylesheet the page had not loaded that matched something on the page was listed. That first pass, with a search of the code for classes styled only in another page's stylesheet, found three that mattered (Accounts' day-by-day table lost its scroll box, so the page scrolled sideways on a phone; the client page's birthday and area forms; and the booking page chat's suggested questions), and all three are fixed. The only rule left is one Pay repeats word for word in its own stylesheet. The app's main script is 414 kB, against 845 kB on main, and a first visit downloads about 1.06 MB against 1.41 MB (about 314 kB against 394 kB compressed). The click-through demo still builds as one file with every page inside it, and in Chromium it opened and moved between Today, Calendar, Business and Settings without errors.

## Automated checks

`npm run check` (lint, the app and server suites and the build) and `npm run format:check` pass. The app suite has 109 files and 933 tests; the server suite 41 files with 279 tests passing and 2 skipped, as before. Tests added or changed with these fixes cover, among others: local and international phone numbers (`src/domain/phone.test.ts`); moving a visit to someone at another location from the desk and from an Ask SALEANE plan; a guest moving their own visit (`server/guestMove.test.ts`) and the test deposit (`server/guestTestDeposit.test.ts`); the booking page's day strip never starting before the studio's today; the receptionist's location prices, opening hours and time buttons; a backup restored without access keys, connections, social accounts or unsent messages; booking page chat never matched to a client; a closed location refusing its general manager everywhere; a regional manager's own requests going to the owner; who can remove a saved card; where gift cards, packages and memberships were sold; rent on the studio's own day; dates written the studio's way; the "Get your studio ready" steps (`src/domain/readiness.test.ts`); the sample studio agreeing with itself; and outgoing connections refusing NAT64 addresses.

## Limits

- Messages, card payments, the AI receptionist and maps stay in test mode. Nothing is sent or charged, and the receptionist's answers come from SALEANE's own rules, not an AI supplier.
- Safari and Firefox were not checked.
- The hosted click-through demo keeps its sample studio in the browser. With the larger sample (about three hundred regulars) that is about 2.2 million characters; if the browser refuses to store it, the demo still works and starts again from the sample on the next visit.
- Book with Google still answers only on this computer, as before.
- The sample studio's clock stands at 10:45 on its sample day, so its finished and coming visits match; real studios use the real clock.
