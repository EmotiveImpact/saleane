# NEXT-12 · Claude behind Ask SALEANE and the receptionist (part 1)

Recorded 10 October 2026 on a local build (Chromium, 127.0.0.1:5173). A studio of its own, not a sample: Linden Studio in Marylebone, with Maya Lind doing a Restorative facial (60 minutes, £95) and two visits that day, Rosa Field at 11:00 and Amara Osei at 14:30. The server was started with Claude switched on and a pretend Anthropic in place of the real one: a stand-in for `fetch`, kept outside the repository, that answers as Anthropic's Messages API documents, waits 900 ms, and answers anything containing "busy" with Anthropic's 529 overloaded error. Everything else (the brain, the engine, the routes, the outbox, the screens) is the code in this change.

## What it does

`SALEANE_AI_PROVIDER=claude` with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set puts Claude behind Ask SALEANE and the AI receptionist (booking page chat, texts and social messages). Unset, both stay in test mode, and a sample studio always does. `lumane/claude.ts` calls the Messages API with the standing instructions and the skills (both cached), the facts for this request (the date and time at the studio, its name and currency, who is asking) and the conversation. Claude's own reply goes back unchanged within a turn, so its reasoning carries through each skill it uses. Each call's cost is worked out from the tokens Anthropic reports and recorded in `assistant_usage`.

A turn starts no further call that, costing as much as its costliest call so far, would pass its share (6 for a question, 4 for a guest message), nor one after its time is up (30 and 20 seconds). Each call carries the whole conversation so far, so the next costs at least as much to read; only a reply longer than any before can still take a turn a little past its share, by at most that extra length (about 2 cents for the longest reply on Sonnet 5.5). A strict upper bound for every call, counted without the cache discount, would stop most two-step answers, so it is not used. When Anthropic is busy SALEANE tries once more, then gives up; it never falls back to another model. Each question's or guest message's share of the allowance (its count, its worst-case spend and its place in the hourly limit) is held before Claude is asked and settled with what it really cost, so requests at the same moment cannot all pass the checks. A hold lasts five minutes: should SALEANE stop mid-request and never settle it, the next look at the studio's usage after that gives its share back, keeping its worst-case spend, since what it cost is unknown. A reply Claude could not finish within its length limit is not used: the request ends as unavailable, with its cost kept. A question Claude cannot answer is not counted against the month, but its cost is. A text or social message is answered after the webhook has been thanked, through the outbox; should SALEANE stop before answering, a message kept in the last ten minutes is answered when it starts again. A repeat of the same message while it is being answered is not answered twice, one guest's messages are answered one at a time in the order they came, and an answer is not sent if someone from the studio replied, or the receptionist was turned off, while Claude was writing it. Fill-ins such as `{{manage_link}}` and `[[booking-link]]` that the AI writes are removed before anything is shown or sent; only SALEANE fills in links.

## What it costs

At Claude Sonnet 5.5's prices ($2 a million tokens in, $10 out, $0.10 read from the cache, $2.50 written to it), counted in the studio's own currency, which counts a pound studio slightly high.

- The cached part of a question is about 3,100 tokens (994 characters of instructions and 29 skills), and of a guest message about 1,200 (909 characters and 7 skills): about 0.03 and 0.01 cents to read, about 0.8 and 0.3 cents when written the first time in five minutes.
- A typical question takes two or three calls: the question and facts (about 400 tokens), what the skills return (a busy day is 1,500 to 3,000 tokens) and short answers (100 to 300 tokens each). That is about 1 to 2 cents, nearer 3 with the cache cold.
- The most one question can cost is a little over its share of 6 cents: no call starts that would pass it at the cost of the costliest call so far, so only a last reply longer than any before can add, about 2 cents at most. A guest message stops the same way at 4 cents. The day's ceiling (150 cents for each location) stops new questions before they start.
- So the 300 questions and 200 guest chats in each location's price come to about $5 to $8 a month in typical use. These figures are worked out from token counts, not measured on a real account.
