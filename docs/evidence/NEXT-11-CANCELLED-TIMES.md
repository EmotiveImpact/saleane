## What it does

When a visit is cancelled and leaves its time free, at least two hours and at most 14 days ahead, the Waitlist page lists the time under Cancelled times. Anyone on the waitlist the time suits is named first, with Offer it to them. Under Might like it are up to five guests who agreed to hear from the studio and have had this kind of treatment, best fit first, each with why in a sentence: due back (or was due back on a date), has seen this professional before, usually comes at that time of day. Of two as likely, the one due back sooner comes first. Guests are left out when they have no email address, have not agreed to hear from the studio, cancelled this time, are already coming that day, already have this treatment booked, are on the waitlist for it, missed a visit in the last three months, were invited to another time in the last week, have not had this kind of treatment, have not been to this location, need a patch test first, or had this treatment too recently.

Ticking guests and choosing Write invitations saves one email each as a draft in the Inbox, with the time, the professional's first name and a link to the booking page with that time already chosen. When nobody is waiting and nobody has been invited, the best three are ticked to start with. When a visit is cancelled and nobody on the waitlist could take the time, SALEANE writes invitations to the best three by itself, also as drafts; Settings, Messages can turn that off. Nothing is sent until someone who can approve messages approves it, and an invitation is not sent once its time is booked or has passed, even after approval. The page counts, over the last 30 days, the times freed by cancellations, those booked again, how many by someone invited or from the waitlist, and what they were worth.

## Not covered

Delivery is unconfigured: approved invitations are recorded in the outbox in test mode, and in the sample studio nothing is sent. Invitations go by email only, to guests who agreed to hear from the studio. A guest's reply arrives in the Inbox like any other; it does not book the time by itself. A time a cancellation frees inside a longer gap is shown as the cancelled visit's time, not every time the gap could hold. Safari and Firefox were not checked.
