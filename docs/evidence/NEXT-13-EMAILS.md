# NEXT-13 · Emails in each studio's own colours (part 1)

Recorded 10 October 2026 on a local build (Chromium, 127.0.0.1:5173), served by the built server with its own content security policy in force. A new studio, Linden Studio in Marylebone (24 Devonshire Place, London W1G 6BX, 020 7946 0000), with one treatment (Restorative facial, 60 minutes, £95), one professional (Maya Lind), Clay as its first colour and Apricot as its second, and one guest, Rosa Field, booked at the desk for Monday 12 October at 10:00.

## What it does

Every email SALEANE sends for a studio now goes as a designed email alongside the plain words: the studio's name at the top with a short line in its second colour, a white card with the subject as its heading, the visit's details in a soft panel of the studio's first colour, the link as a button, the studio's name to sign off, and its address, phone and email at the foot. Nothing is added or reworded. The design is laid out from the same stored words, so an email app that shows only the plain version shows the same message. A line about why the guest hears from the studio (the keep-in-touch line) moves to the foot in small type. The button's words come from the message itself ("View, add to your calendar or cancel", "Rate your visit and Maya") or, when the words before a link are a sentence, from what the link does ("Leave a review", "Book a visit", "Book this time").

The button colour is the studio's first colour, deepened only as far as it takes for white words on it to reach WCAG AA (4.5:1). Clay, `#a35f4b`, goes out as `#955948`; SALEANE's own sage goes out as `#59796a`. Account emails (choose a new password, confirm your email) go in SALEANE's own look, never a studio's, with the link on a button. Texts are unchanged.

Birthday, "we miss you", "due back" and news a studio marks as marketing go on Postmark's broadcast stream, kept apart from visit messages, as Postmark asks. `POSTMARK_BROADCAST_STREAM` must name that stream by its Stream ID, copied from Postmark; with Postmark chosen and it unset, the server will not start and names the missing setting, rather than guess a stream Postmark may not have.

Settings, Messages has a new section, How your emails look, for owners and managers. It shows a booking confirmation, a reminder, an after-the-visit email and a birthday email, written by the same code as the real ones, for a made-up guest, Alex, on the studio's own first treatment with its first professional, tomorrow at 10:00. Nothing is saved or sent for a look, and no private link is made, so the button to manage the visit shows but goes nowhere. In the Sent list each email has See the email, which opens it as the guest saw it, in the studio's colours today. Practitioners see neither, as they have no Sent list.

A "due back" email from a studio used to show its booking link as `{{book_link}}` in the Sent list; it now shows the studio's booking page address.

## Not covered

Delivery is unconfigured: in test mode the outbox records each designed email and sends nothing. The design was checked in Chromium only. It was not checked in Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail or Yahoo, where some things differ: Outlook on Windows shows square corners and its own fonts, and email apps that do not load web fonts show Georgia and the device's own sans-serif in place of Newsreader and Figtree. Emails carry no photos. A studio's own sender, other email services and a Marketing section come in the next parts of NEXT-13.
