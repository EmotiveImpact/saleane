# NEXT-16 · How the AI speaks, and voice notes after a visit

Recorded 10 October 2026 on a local build (Chromium, 127.0.0.1:5173), in studios of their own, not samples. For the live journeys the server was started with Claude, OpenAI's voice and OpenAI's transcription switched on, and a pretend Anthropic and a pretend OpenAI in place of the real ones, kept outside the repository: the pretend OpenAI "hears" a fixed voice note whatever is recorded, and the pretend Claude sorts the words it is given into its two parts. The browser's microphone and recorder were stood in for, as a test browser has neither. The typed journey was walked again with the server in test mode. Everything else (the settings, the routes, the metering and the screens) is the code in this change.

## What it does

**How your AI speaks.** Settings, AI and voice has a panel for the owner or a manager: a tone (Warm and friendly, Calm and polished, or Bright and cheerful), each with a sample answer; up to 400 characters on how the studio likes to speak; and Ask SALEANE's voice, from the six on the shortlist, with Vesper as SALEANE's own. Reception sees the choices without changing them. The AI receptionist answers guests in the tone; in test mode its greeting, the times it offers, the time it holds and its thanks use each tone's own words. Ask SALEANE writes what it drafts for clients in it. The studio's words reach the AI quoted, as a note on style only, after the rules: they cannot change what it may do, and it still says it is an AI. In Ask SALEANE, anyone who can talk has a Voice list, "The studio's (Willow)" first, and their next talk uses their choice. Nobody can choose a voice for someone else.

**Voice notes after a visit.** On a professional's own day, a visit whose guest has arrived (or that is complete) shows After the visit. They tap Speak a note and talk for up to two minutes, or type what they would say. SALEANE returns a note for the studio and aftercare for the guest, each in a box to change, with what was heard above them; nothing is saved until they press Save. The note joins the guest's history as "Note after the visit"; the aftercare is kept on the visit and joins the treatment's own advice in the aftercare email as "A note from Maya: …", even for a treatment with no advice of its own. Saved aftercare shows on the visit with Remove. Once the visit's aftercare email has gone, or two days have passed since checkout, or the guest has no email, it says plainly that the aftercare is kept on the visit only and no email will carry it. Reception and managers can write up any visit at their studio that went ahead, from the visit's panel on Today or the calendar; a professional only their own.

In test mode the words are sorted by themselves (a sentence reading like advice for the guest goes to the aftercare), free, and nothing leaves SALEANE; voice notes are not offered. With Claude, typed words are written up by Claude. With talking switched on as well, a voice note is written down by OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-transcribe first, and the recording is not kept. Each write-up counts as one Ask SALEANE question; its worst cost (3 cents for Claude, plus 3 for a recording) is held before anything is sent, and the cost OpenAI's token counts and Claude's reply report is what is kept. A write-up that fails, or a recording in which nothing was heard, says so plainly and is not counted as a question, though what it cost still is.

## What it costs

A write-up by Claude is about 1 cent (a few hundred words in, a short reply out) and is held at 3. OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-transcribe is $1.25 a million audio tokens in and $5 a million text tokens out (OpenAI's price on 10 October 2026); The model page does not say how many tokens a minute of speech uses, so what a real note costs will be known from the first real notes. Until then each is held at the most its limits allow (16,000 tokens in and 2,000 out), 3 cents, and counted from the tokens OpenAI reports. Each write-up uses one of the location's 300 Ask SALEANE questions a month. These figures are worked out from published prices, not measured on a real account.

## Not covered

Not run against a real OpenAI or Anthropic account: no key was used. Real speech and a real microphone were not exercised, so how well OpenAI writes down a noisy salon is untested. Safari records as MP4, which the server accepts but which was not tried; Safari and Firefox were not checked. Messages, card payments and everything else stay in test mode.
