## Not covered

Google Calendar reads a diary from the internet, so it cannot reach a SALEANE that runs only on this computer; Apple Calendar on this computer can. No real Google or Apple calendar was subscribed: the files were checked by parsing them. The diary only shows; nothing made in Google or Apple comes back to SALEANE. How often a calendar app checks is up to the app (Google every few hours). Calendar apps keep what they last read when a link stops until it is removed there. Safari and Firefox were not checked.
