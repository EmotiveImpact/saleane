# SALEANE version roadmap

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Every roadmap feature from V1 to V5 is built locally in test mode; nothing is deployed. Further items that can still be built locally are queued as NEXT-01 to NEXT-20. What remains before release is listed under [What's left before release](#whats-left-before-release). Milestones are outcome gates, not promised dates. The user authorised consolidation into main on 6 October 2026. This does not authorise deployment or future automatic merges.

## Current position

**0.2.0 — Connected foundation:** Vite, React and TypeScript; custom visual system; browser-local Today, Calendar, client directory and timeline, branded booking, simulated checkout/rebooking and derived business reporting. Executable booking, ledger and persistence rules; fictional UK and US workspaces. See [verification evidence](evidence/VERIFICATION.md).

The previous **v0.1** was a cloud discovery pack and HTML concept, referenced in the source conversation. It is not a Git release. The entire visible source conversation and the original PRD attachment preview have now been read; its readable text is archived. ZIP download attempts did not return bytes. The original generated visual board was subsequently inspected directly. After the first new studies were rejected, the revised studies and 0.2.1 app follow its light-sidebar, serif and photographic direction. Original image bytes remain unimported.

**0.2.2 — Appointment changes (locally verified):** confirmed-visit rescheduling, cancellation/missed history with retained deposits, calendar navigation/selection and a persistent panel heading. Local lint, 72 tests, build and targeted browser journeys passed; see [0.2.2 evidence](evidence/VERIFICATION_0.2.2.md). These changes exercise the daily loop in fictional local state and do not satisfy server, payment or communications gates.

**1.0.0-rc.1 — Local V1 candidate:** the user authorised a team build and selected local-first operation with live services unconfigured. Local account/studio authority, editable operating configuration, guest holds and exact-room allocation, manual money, unsent drafts, bounded client import and backup/restore are now implemented. See [local contract](V1_LOCAL_CONTRACT.md) and [candidate evidence](evidence/VERIFICATION_1.0.0-rc.1.md). The candidate version identifies a reviewable local app; it does not waive any stable production 1.0 gate.

Decided on 9 October 2026: the owner said yes to the price and to the five moving-in answers.

- **Price:** £59 a month in the UK and $69 in the US, for each location (raised from £49 and $59 by the owner on 10 October 2026, with Marketing included). ## Competitive position

Reviewed on 6 October 2026 against Mindbody ([comparison](https://claude.ai/artifact/HcnwCc2gJEZb7GmwGpcifU)). SALEANE shares the category's basic jobs (booking, payments, client records) but is not becoming Mindbody, which is built around classes, a commission marketplace, tiered pricing and chains. The closest rivals are Mangomint and Boulevard; Fresha and Phorest matter in the UK. The user accepted these changes to the plan:

1. **Memberships and packages lead V1.5.** Recurring income is the main reason studios stay with their software. Build them first in V1.5, with the existing liability, redemption and refund rules.
2. **A narrow missed-call text-back arrives in V1.5**, well before the full V4 concierge. When a call is missed, the client receives a consented text with the studio's booking link. It needs a qualified SMS provider, purpose/channel consent and delivery receipts; it never claims a message was sent without a receipt.
3. **No marketplace remains a firm rule.** No consumer marketplace, commission on clients or competitor promotion.
4. **Fair discovery is an idea to validate:** booking straight from Google search and an Instagram booking button, both landing on the studio's own branded booking.
5. **One promise, shown in ordinary work:** "SALEANE helps a small, high-touch studio run every visit well, and turn good visits into lasting client relationships." Every feature should show up in one of four moments: before the visit (the right practitioner and room, preferences easy to find), during the day (delays, changes and open tasks visible without searching), at checkout (payment, balances and the next visit connected) and afterwards (who needs attention, why, and the permitted next step).
6. **A signature workflow to aim for: from cancellation to filled slot.** A cancellation identifies the free practitioner and room, finds suitable consenting clients, lets the team approve an invitation and measures whether the slot was filled. Built locally (NEXT-11, 10 October 2026): the waitlist comes first, then guests who agreed to hear from the studio and might like the time, each invitation drafted for approval with a link that books that time, and the page counts times filled again and by whom. Delivery is unconfigured until a message service is connected.
7. **Keep navigation focused.** Today, Calendar, Clients, Inbox and Business stay the main sections; new modules live inside them rather than adding to the sidebar.
8. **The test that matters:** can one real studio run its day with less effort, fewer mistakes and better continuity for its clients than with its current software? Mindbody stays a serious benchmark, but the pilot is judged on this.
9. **Gaps closed on 7 October 2026.** A review against Fresha, Phorest, Boulevard and Vagaro (from general knowledge of those apps, not a fresh check of each) added the items the user accepted: gift cards and vouchers, patch test tracking, and birthday and "we miss you" messages to V1.5; rotas and time off, chair and room rental, tips, supplier reordering and a direct Xero and QuickBooks link to V2; filling quiet gaps and staff targets to V3; Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook messages, Book with Google and an Instagram book button to V4; shared brand rules and Zapier-style connections to V5. Later the same day the user added regional manager, general manager and CEO roles, regions with approvals, branch checklists and branch and client-area maps to V5 (rather than a new V6), and local and seasonal awareness for the assistant manager to V3. Patch tests move out of the clinical track because they are a salon record and reminder.
10. **Steer away from** class timetables and gym features, feature sprawl, locked pricing tiers, long contracts and exit fees.

## Ideas to validate, rather than commitments

Booking from Google search and an Instagram button that land on the studio's own branded booking; a waiting-list invitation that starts with a real empty slot; an owner brief that explains a shortfall with receipt-level evidence; a guest booking link that retains the business’s identity; a consultation that carries its version into a visit; a small group’s shared services with location-specific pricing; a stock suggestion tied to booked consumption.

The sequence matters: a polished inbox without consent and delivery history is not relationship ownership. An AI conversation without authorised commands is not an operating system.

## Deferred decisions required for a live pilot

1. Select the first non-clinical pilot business and map its actual hours, resources and booking policy.
2. Choose authentication, hosting, database and payment providers using UK/US residency, availability and cost evidence. No vendor is deployed or approved by this foundation. Hosting is parked by the owner (7 October 2026) and undecided.
3. Decide client identity/verification and reviewed duplicate-resolution policy. Never auto-merge on email.
4. Decide VAT/sales-tax classifications, gratuity allocation, cancellation/refund treatment and receipts with qualified advisers.
5. Validate the wordmark and naming with target users; complete trademark/domain clearance separately.

Every release updates the changelog, PRD status matrix and evidence. Every provider-dependent claim names the environment in which it was verified.

## Foundation sequence and immediate continuation

Research v0.2 → PRD product bible → UX architecture → three visual directions → connected prototype → real build is the governing sequence. Research/design overlap and discoveries revise the contract. The context reconciliation, screen map and visual comparisons close gaps in the first repository foundation; they do not certify production readiness.

The experimental server draft at `f73d084` is superseded by the verified Node/SQLite candidate. Its ancestry is retained during consolidation with an explicit history-only merge; the Fastify/PGlite/Postgres draft and its additional dependencies are not installed or substituted into the application. The original branch remains available for archaeology. See [AI handoff](AI_HANDOFF.md).
