# Photographs for "Set up your studio"

Each screen of "Set up your studio" shows one house photograph beside the form. Today it uses photographs already in the library (`src/design/photos.ts`), so nothing is missing. These prompts are for a fresh set made for the setup screens, with a good mix of people, if the owner would like them.

## How to use them

- Paste one prompt at a time into ChatGPT (or another image tool) and ask for one photograph.
- Ask for a **portrait** picture, **2:3**, at least **1600 × 2400 pixels**.
- On a computer the photograph fills a tall column with the studio's name written over its top half, so the prompts keep the top half plain and light and put the subject in the lower half. On a phone it becomes a short band, so the subject should sit near the middle, left to right.
- Save each as WebP (or PNG) with the file name given, and send them back in the project chat. They will go in `public/images/library/setup/` and replace the current photograph on that screen.
- All of them are fictional and AI-made, like the rest of the library. They are never shown as a real studio's premises or people.

## The house style (already inside every prompt)

Editorial photography for a calm, premium beauty and wellness studio. Bright, airy and sunlit, with soft natural daylight through linen or sheer curtains. Warm off-white walls, limewash plaster, pale oak, travertine, linen, ceramic and dried botanicals, with touches of muted sage green and dusty rose. Light, uncluttered and serene, with gentle shadows. Shot on a medium-format camera with a 50mm lens and shallow depth of field, in natural colour. Real, natural-looking people, never posed stock-photo smiles. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks, no readable screens. Not dark, moody, candlelit, neon or night-time.
