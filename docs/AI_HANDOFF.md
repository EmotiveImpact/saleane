## What actually works

Version **1.0.0-rc.1** connects Today → Calendar → Client → Appointment → manual Checkout → Rebook → Business intelligence. There are two modes: the preserved fictional browser demo with a fixed 6 October 2026 date, and authenticated local studios with current venue time and SQLite authority.

Local accounts, tenant memberships, manual invitations, treatments/qualified professionals/resources, hours/closures/tax setup, public availability with exact-room holds, the visit lifecycle, client timeline, manual receipts/deposits/refunds, unsent drafts, bounded CSV imports and backup/restore are implemented. Empty UK/GBP and US/USD studios can be created through `/account`; a new studio starts closed on Sunday with online booking off, opens on "Set up your studio" (`/setup`), and Today lists what is left to set up. Professional catalogue entries do not confer login access by themselves.

Merged on top of that, all built locally and in test mode where a provider would be needed:

- LOCAL-01 to LOCAL-05 and OFF-01: practitioner access ("Your day"), guest manage links (cancel, and since 9 October move), privacy and the data lifecycle, schedule administration, a qualification pass, and the read-only offline day sheet.
- Pilot basics (PILOT-01 to 04): visit messages, saved cards, card payments and deposits in test mode, switching from another system, one published price.
- V1.5: gift cards, packages and memberships, patch tests, forms, consultations, waitlist (with pass-on), due back and aftercare, birthday and "we miss you", reviews, missed-call text-back, the Inbox.
- V2: stock and retail at the till, commissions, tips, rota and time off, chair and room rent, supplier orders, accountant exports, several locations.
- V3 core: Ask SALEANE (morning brief, warnings, booking and cancelling by chat, cover plans), smart insights and specials.

Times are written in UTC from the studio's time zone, the UID is a hash of the visit's ID (`stableUid('diary', id)`), and a practitioner's diary reaches as far as `PractitionerView`. A restore stops every link (`diaryLinksStopped`), and stopping a link is allowed while a studio is read only.

- NEXT-12, part 1: Claude behind Ask SALEANE and the receptionist (`lumane/claude.ts`, `server/ai.ts`). `aiFromEnv` reads `SALEANE_AI_PROVIDER`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `SALEANE_AI_MODEL` and `SALEANE_AI_PRICES` and stops the server without a key or a known price; `ctx.ai.brainFor(state, use)` gives undefined (the test-mode brain) for a sample studio. The brain is stateless: Claude's own content blocks travel back as `raw` on the assistant message, so thinking and tool use are returned unchanged within a turn. Instructions and skills are cached; the per-request facts (`askFacts`, `receptionFacts`) go in a second, uncached system block. `runTurn` starts no call that would take `costMinor` past `worstCaseMinor` if it cost as much as the costliest call so far, nor one past `timeLimitMs`, and a `BrainError` ends the turn as `unavailable` (cost recorded, nothing counted). Texts and social messages are answered after the webhook returns when the AI is live (`answerSoon` in `server/app.ts`, deduplicated in flight by `answering`); `answerMissed` runs when the server starts with a live AI and answers kept texts and social messages from the last ten minutes that have no `reception:` outbox row; those the allowance refused (a stopped request's hold may still count) are tried once more after `missedAgainMs`, by default `HOLD_SECONDS` plus 15 seconds. Every AI request's share is held before the call and settled after it (`reserve`/`settle` in `lumane/allowance.ts`, `holding`/`settleUse` in `server/aiUsage.ts`, which replaced `receptionistUsage.ts`); the turn is given the usage from before the hold, so its own check agrees. Holds are kept in `usage.holds` with an `until` (five minutes by default); `storedUsage` runs `reclaim`, which gives back any hold past its time (amount returned, worst-case spend kept), and a settle that finds no hold counts the amount again only if it counts. A Claude reply stopped by `max_tokens` or a context limit throws `BrainError('failed')`, so the turn ends as unavailable. One guest's texts and social messages are answered one at a time (`inTurn`), and the outbox insert re-checks that the team has not answered and the receptionist is still on. AI text is stripped of `{{…}}` and `[[…]]` before use. No migration. Proven only against a pretend Anthropic. Part 2 (OpenAI's live voice, with Claude thinking through client delegation) is next.

## Invariants that must survive

- Validate membership, command permissions, expected revision and scoped request keys server-side, in transactions. Never accept client role claims or use UI hiding as access control.
- Existing whole-studio BusinessState reads are unsuitable for practitioner access. Practitioners receive only the `PractitionerView` from `src/domain/practitioner.ts` via `server/projections.ts`, and only `practitionerRefusal`-approved commands; never widen this by frontend filtering or fabricated empty collections. V5 regional managers (role `regional`, scoped to one region by `memberships.area_id`, migration 20) likewise receive only the `AreaView` from `src/domain/area.ts` and only `areaRefusal`-approved commands (branch visits, and raising and deciding requests for a sign-off, at their own locations; deciding only when the request was routed to their region). Which regions have a regional manager comes from `memberships` on the server and is injected into `request-raise`; a client-sent list is overwritten. V5 general managers (role `general`, scoped to one location by `memberships.location_id`, migration 21) read the studio record through `branchView` (no pay rates; only their location's branch visits and requests) and may use only `GENERAL_ACTIONS` (operations.ts). Every command is checked by `branchRefusal` before it runs and `branchChangeRefusal` after (no visit added or changed outside their location); card payments, card refunds and guest links check the visit's location too. They are manager-like on other routes, so a new route that changes something at a location must check `org.locationId` for them. `orgFor` refuses a regional manager on every route unless the route passes `{ area: true }`; only the workspace read and command routes do, so new routes stay closed to them by default.
- Keep immutable booked service/price/duration/buffer terms, original appointment/client IDs, exact held rooms and linked history. Public holds and desk bookings share availability. Check venue civil time, elapsed duration and DST.
- Operator uncertain requests retain SHA-256-scoped fingerprint → request UUID mappings in tab session storage (200 limit), without command payloads. Storage failure/corruption blocks writes. Reload must not mint a new key for an uncertain operation.
- Guest confirmation retains its exact submitted contact/consent and hold snapshot in tab storage until a definitive outcome. Unknown results remain protected even after hold expiry; do not enable a second booking/edit while recovery is unresolved. Closing the tab is a documented recovery boundary.
- A late async response must not replace a different studio. Revision conflicts load current records without silently resubmitting the initiating draft.
- Browser demo reset must advance persisted revision rather than returning to zero. It is still browser-local storage, not a multi-user transactional authority.
- Money is in minor units with provenance and immutable receipts. Manual payment/refund records explicitly acknowledge already-performed external activity. Cancelled visits retain deposit history; no fabricated fee/refund.
- V5 client areas keep only the first half of a postcode (`client.postcodeArea`, normalised by `postcodeArea` in `src/domain/clientAreas.ts`; a full postcode is cut before it is stored). `clientAreas` names only areas with at least `AREA_MIN_GROUP` (3) clients and folds the rest into "Other areas"; keep that floor in any new view, and never place individual clients on a map. The map (`src/domain/areaMap.ts`, `AreaMap`) draws only `mapAreas` output, which applies the same floor; `mapReady` is true only for sample studios until a real map service adapter exists.
- V5 branch map (`branchMap` in `src/domain/branchMap.ts`, `BranchMap`): places each location with `placeCentre` and shows only `wholeBusiness` figures. The regional manager's `AreaView.map` is built with their region's location ids; keep it to those, and keep it null when `mapReady` is false.
- V5 brands together (`GET /api/group`, `src/domain/brandGroup.ts`, `BrandsTogether` on /account) reads every studio the account owns and returns totals only, from `wholeBusiness`. Keep it to `role === 'owner'` memberships and never return client, visit or receipt records from it; totals are added only within one currency and never mix sample with real studios. Each brand stays its own studio; nothing is shared between them.
- V5 benchmarking (`GET /api/workspaces/:id/benchmark`, `server/routes/benchmark.ts`, `src/domain/benchmark.ts`): only owners turn `operations.benchmark.sharing` on, and only a sharing studio gets a comparison. Peers are other sharing studios in the same region and of the same kind (sample or real), grouped by owner (`perOwner`; the requesting owner's studios are left out). Keep the response to the rounded (`coarse`) spread of `studioMeasures`, return `comparison: null` below `BENCHMARK_MIN_STUDIOS` (10) owners, and never return a peer's id, name or count.

Keep every v1 endpoint free of client names unless it needs `clients:read`.

- Messages passed on from the booking page chat always wait unmatched for the team; never match a chat sender to a client by an email or number they typed. Texts from a number already on file still match.
- A newly added Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp account is stored unconfirmed (`unconfirmed:<orgId>:<accountId>`) until the owner proves it is theirs, and message routing never matches an unconfirmed id. Once one studio confirms an account, no other studio can add it.
- Requests record who raised them (`raisedById`, from the session, never from the browser). A regional manager's own request goes to the owner, and they cannot decide a request they raised.
- Only the owner and managers, or a general manager for a client who visits their location, may remove a saved card (`mayRemoveSavedCard`). `orgFor` refuses a general manager whose location has closed on every route.
