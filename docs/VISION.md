# SALEANE vision

Version 0.2 · 6 October 2026 · product direction, not a claim of shipped capability.

Saleane should become the operating system for modern beauty, spa and wellness businesses in the United Kingdom and United States. It should remember the whole relationship, make the working day dependable, and help an owner make better decisions without creating more administrative work.

The company and platform are **Saleane**. The canonical brand mark is **SALEANE**, with no accent. “Operating system” describes the ambition; “Saleane OS” is not a second brand. Saleane Clinical is a separate future capability and compliance boundary.

## The end state

A solo practitioner and a multi-brand group should recognise the same product. A client has a durable identity inside an organisation; their visits connect services, people, spaces, money, preferences and communication. A cancelled visit can expose capacity. That capacity can inform a considered invitation. A successful return can update retention and cash planning. Nothing needs to be copied into a separate marketing database to make this work.

Seven connected domains form the operating model:

| Domain               | The owner’s question                                 | Durable connection                                               |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relationships        | Who is this person, and what matters to them?        | Client, explicit relationships, visit and consent history        |
| Calendar & resources | Can we deliver this promise?                         | Staff capability, working hours, location, room, time            |
| Money                | What was earned, collected, owed or refunded?        | Price snapshot, sale, deposit, payment, adjustment               |
| Inventory            | Can we provide and sell what was promised?           | Product, location stock movement, service consumption, sale line |
| Communications       | What should we say, through which permitted channel? | Client, purpose-specific consent, conversation, delivery event   |
| People               | Who can do the work, and how is it rewarded?         | Membership, capability, availability, commission rule            |
| Intelligence         | What should we notice and do next?                   | Traceable facts, decision proposals, approved commands           |

This is a domain model, not seven disconnected tools. A timeline is a useful view of that model; it is not the database’s authority.

## Work backwards

- V5 groups and integrations require organisation and location scopes in V1 IDs and contracts.
- V4 concierge needs safe booking, communication and payment commands long before voice is connected.
- V3 intelligence needs defined metrics, events and actor attribution rather than charts assembled from unrelated totals.
- V2 inventory and commissions need immutable service-price and sale-line snapshots in the first checkout.
- V1.5 relationship ownership needs purpose-specific consent and explicit graph edges in the first client model.
- V1 running the day needs a reliable appointment promise before adding growth automation.

The current v0.2 foundation tests the vocabulary and connected journeys with fictional, browser-local data. It gives the next production slice a concrete interface and executable rules. Authentication, a server database and providers remain future work.

## Product doctrine

1. **Remember the person.** A booking is a moment in a relationship. Email is contact data, not a permanent identity or a safe automatic merge key.
2. **Make promises that can be kept.** A professional and a suitable space must both be available. Buffers, closures and time zones are part of availability.
3. **Give money a clear history.** Deposits are part-payments. Earned revenue, cash collected, gratuities and liabilities have different meanings.
4. **Earn the right to automate.** Suggestions show their evidence. Sending messages, moving appointments and collecting money require appropriate authority and a reviewable action.
5. **Feel dependable.** Weight comes from legible type, stable structure, restrained colour and honest state. Dense operational information should remain calm.
6. **Let the business own the relationship.** Branded booking stays focused on the selected business; a marketplace is not the core product.
7. **Keep clinical work deliberate.** No diagnosis, clinical chart, medication, treatment photograph or health-data inference enters core Saleane by accident.

## Who we serve first

First pilot: an owner-led, non-clinical studio with two to five professionals, a small service catalogue and shared treatment rooms. This exposes genuine resource and relationship complexity while keeping onboarding achievable. A solo practitioner must still find the product straightforward. Five-to-ten-person businesses follow; multi-location groups validate the end-state architecture later.

Success is a full working loop: a client books, the business keeps the promise, the visit is paid for accurately, the relationship is remembered, and the next visit has a place.

## What excellence means

The product should be recognisable without its logo, useful without training, and trustworthy when something fails. User testing must include a busy receptionist, an owner reconciling receipts, a professional with limited client access, and a customer booking on a phone with assistive technology.

The ambition is durable. Versions remain bounded. We do not call an attractive demo a pilot, or call a connected pilot production-ready before its release gates pass.
