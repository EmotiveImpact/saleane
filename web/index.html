Questions studios ask

Who is SALEANE made for?

Independent hair, beauty, spa and wellness studios, from one person working alone to teams across several locations.

Can my clients book online?

Yes. Guests book from your own booking page, pay a deposit if you ask for one, and can move or cancel their visit from a private link.

Can I bring my existing clients?

Yes. We move you in from your old system for free, including visits already booked. If your old file has no marketing permission for someone, we never assume it.

Can I try it without a card?

Yes. You get 14 days free, and we don't ask for a card to start.

How does pricing work for my team?

Each location covers five people who take bookings. Each extra person is £9 a month, and reception and managers are always free.

Does it work in the UK and the US?

Yes. Each studio has its own currency, time zone and spelling.
